Add it to your MCP client


    

    
The first time your client starts the server, it installs the small connector this extension talks to (no admin rights, no open ports). Requires Node.js 18 or newer.

  


  

    
Good to know

    
The agent only works in tabs inside the MCP tab group. Pages it drives glow blue and show a Stop automation button. Click this extension's toolbar icon any time to come back here.

    
Something not working? Run npx -y browser-driver-mcp doctor — it checks every step and says how to fix it.
